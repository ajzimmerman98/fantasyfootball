Boone's values come from Justin Boone's rest-of-season trade value charts on Yahoo. FantasyCalc's come from real fantasy trades, rescaled so its top player is 100 (close to Boone's scale). ▲/▼ = change since last week: Justin Boone's previous weekly chart, or last week's FantasyCalc values (until a week of history is saved, FantasyCalc's own 30-day change). Rank mode shows the position rank and places moved; ranks are Justin Boone's own, or by value for FantasyCalc. Each league uses its own format: PPR, half-PPR or standard, 1QB or superflex, and (for FantasyCalc) its number of teams. The same choice sets the TV numbers on the Lineup tab.
Rest of season: score = starting lineup + depth. Starting lineup is the best lineup each roster can field (QB, RB, WR, TE and flex spots) at full value. Depth adds the six best bench players at 35%, 30%, 25%, 20%, 15% and 10% of their value. IR and taxi players don't count; kickers and defenses aren't valued. FantasyPros ranks are turned into values on a curve (#1 = 100; QBs scaled lower in 1QB leagues) so they can be added up. Each league uses its own format.

This week: score = projected points of the best starting lineup each team can field this week, kickers and defenses included; players who are out or on bye don't count, and the bench doesn't either. Sleeper projections use Sleeper's projected points; Justin Boone and FantasyPros ranks are turned into estimated points on a curve for each position (e.g. FLEX #1 ≈ 24, #50 ≈ 11.5).

Playoff odds (Sleeper leagues): the rest of the regular season is played out 10,000 times on the league's real schedule, starting from the current standings. Each team's score is drawn around its strength: this week, Sleeper's projection for its best lineup (so byes and injuries count); later weeks, its points per game so far pulled toward the league average (about halfway after 7 games), since part of anyone's scoring is luck. Each simulated season also shifts every team's strength by a random amount (about ±7 points a game) for injuries, trades and pickups nobody can predict. Seeds go by wins, then points for; the league's playoff spots, byes and median games are used, and each run finishes with the playoff bracket for the title odds. Divisions aren't taken into account.
Sleeper leagues, back through every season the league was renewed. Records, points and head-to-head count regular-season games; playoff wins and losses come from the winners bracket. The current season counts finished weeks only. All-play = your record if you'd played every team every week; luck = actual win % minus all-play win %. Finished seasons are saved on this device. ESPN history isn't supported yet.

Draft recap: grades rank managers by the points their picks scored that season; steals and busts compare each player's draft slot with where he finished in points among drafted players (keepers left out; busts come from the first quarter of the draft). Trades and pickups are judged by points the new players scored in their new team's starting lineup for the rest of that season (regular season). Draft picks and FAAB in trades are listed, not valued.
Schedule from ESPN, forecasts from Open-Meteo. Wind, rain and snow cover the ~3 hours of game time. Retractable roofs are usually closed in bad weather.
